# Citation Policy

## Metadata and authority
Version 1.0, English edition; scope: one project. Based on the documented source-authority hierarchy. Approved for this personal documentation package by Diego Aguirre as self-declared owner; not a third-party citation standard.

## Evidence types
1. **Attached raw originals:** establish the preserved wording, not the truth of all claims.
2. **Research report/adoption record:** evidence of what the report states and its specifically limited personal approval.
3. **GitHub, Anthropic, OWASP, and other external sources:** references cited *by the report*; their full historical states are **not independently revalidated** by this ZIP.
4. **Professional inference and technical conventions:** label explicitly, without claiming an author stated them.
5. **Later packaging authorization:** Diego Aguirre's follow-up approval applies to this English personal memory package; do not attribute it to the historical Chat 1 original.

## Citation identifiers
Include `raw/.../filename.md`, a section and/or line span where applicable, and SHA-256 when identifying byte-level evidence. External references require a URL, issuing party, available publication and access dates, and a snapshot/commit when known. Use `NOT_RECORDED` for unavailable dates. Reading a report containing a URL is **not** the same as independently checking that URL.

## Evidence strength
Directly checked original wording > a report's statement about an outside source > reasoned professional inference > assumption. A substantive decision requires directly attributable approval or a scoped pre-existing record. Do not presume signatures by outside parties.

## Reference format
Use relative Markdown links to source files plus `§` for sections and, when useful, line numbers. Record SHA-256 for raw-byte provenance. Search indexes are **derived**, not authoritative primary sources.

## Corrections
If a citation and original conflict, record the issue and a corrective event; fix derived materials without changing raw originals. Do not assume third-party update dates or silently overwrite past decisions.
